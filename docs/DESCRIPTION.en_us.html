Buena Mono is a variable monospace typeface designed for writers, developers, and anyone who works with text on screen. It prioritizes readability in extended prose while maintaining the technical precision required for code editing, terminal use, and data display.

The typeface offers 8 weights from Thin (100) to ExtraBold (800) on a continuous variable weight axis, in Roman and Italic styles (a 10-degree slant). All weights and styles share a fixed advance width of 618 units at 1000 UPM, ensuring perfect character alignment across columns, tables, and indented structures.

Buena Mono includes 4,886 glyphs covering 3,870 Unicode codepoints across Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, Braille Patterns, Mathematical Operators, Arrows, Phonetic Extensions, and more, with support for over 600 languages. The character set includes full box drawing (128 glyphs), block elements (32 glyphs), and native Powerline symbols, making it terminal-ready without font patching. Currency symbols, mathematical operators, arrows, and enclosed alphanumerics provide broad coverage for technical and scientific writing.

The font ships with 50 OpenType features. Standard and coding ligatures combine 175 multi-character sequences for arrows, operators, and common programming patterns. Twelve stylistic sets offer alternate letterforms for a, f, g, i, j, l, r, y, and zero, drawn from a survey of leading monospace typefaces. Additional features include small caps, oldstyle figures, case- sensitive forms, fractions, superscripts, subscripts, and localized forms for Turkish and Azerbaijani.

Markdown-aware design distinguishes heading markers, list bullets, emphasis characters, and blockquote indicators at a glance. A low stroke contrast ratio between 1.1:1 and 1.3:1 maintains legibility across all weights and screen sizes, from 9px terminal minimums to 24px display use.
